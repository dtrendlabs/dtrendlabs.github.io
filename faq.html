Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our apps, pricing, privacy, and support.

🌐 General

DTrend Services is a mobile product studio that designs, develops, and publishes Android apps. We build apps for everyday use — productivity, finance, health, education, and more — available for users to download directly from the Google Play Store.

Yes! Most of our apps are completely free with full core functionality. No hidden charges, no forced upgrades. Some apps offer optional premium features through a Pro subscription or one-time purchase, but the free versions are fully functional on their own.

📱 Apps & Features

Most of our apps work fully offline. Apps like TaskFlow Pro, FitPulse, and QuickNote don't require an internet connection for core functionality. Features like cloud sync naturally require connectivity.

Our apps typically support Android 7.0 (Nougat) and above, which covers 95%+ of active Android devices. Specific requirements are listed on each app's Play Store page.

We ship updates regularly — typically every 2-4 weeks. Updates include bug fixes, new features, performance improvements, and compatibility with the latest Android versions. You can enable auto-updates on Google Play to always have the latest version.

🔒 Privacy & Security

We collect the minimum data necessary for the app to function (e.g., account email for cloud sync). We never sell your data to third parties. Each app has its own privacy policy linked on its Play Store listing and on our website.

Yes. Apps that handle sensitive data (like VaultGuard) use AES-256 encryption. All data transmitted to our servers uses TLS encryption. We follow industry best practices for data security.

🛟 Support

Visit our Contact page and select "Bug Report." Include the app name, your Android version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots or screen recordings are super helpful!

We aim to respond within 24 hours on business days. Pro subscribers get priority support with faster response times. Critical issues (crashes, data loss) are escalated immediately.

We're primarily a product company, not an agency. However, if you have an interesting app idea, we'd love to hear about it! Reach out via our Contact page and select "Partnership."
